# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Primary users are operations managers working inside a SaaS platform that spans site monitoring, HVAC device oversight, and EMS operational decision support.

They work primarily from desktop environments, need quick access to site-level context, and are responsible for understanding operational status without getting buried in control-room noise. Their job is to move from portfolio awareness to site-specific action with clarity and confidence.

## Product Purpose

The EMS-Demo platform supports operational visibility and decision support across distributed sites, HVAC assets, and energy-management workflows.

For the site integration surface, success means an operations manager can start from a map-based portfolio view, identify a specific site, understand its current device and system status, and access focused EMS workflows such as generation forecasting and optimization, consumption forecasting and optimization, sell-power forecasting and management, bidirectional charging workflows, business-mode management forecasting, mobile bidirectional charger workflows, and energy-resource commercialization inventory management.

The product should help users make better operational decisions through clean, prioritized interfaces rather than overwhelming them with raw telemetry or dense control-room dashboards.

## Brand Personality

Calm, intelligent, operational.

## Anti-references

This should not look like an AI-generated SCADA control room packed with numbers, charts, and visual noise.

It should also avoid generic blue SaaS-dashboard tropes, decorative futurism, and dense monitoring layouts that make every screen feel equally urgent.

## Design Principles

- Start from site context, then narrow into action.
- Prioritize operational judgment over telemetry spectacle.
- Keep visualizations sparse, useful, and decision-oriented.
- Make complex EMS workflows feel structured and approachable.
- Maintain a clean product surface that signals trust without looking sterile.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA.

Support reduced motion preferences, maintain strong text and status contrast, and avoid color-only status communication. The primary experience is desktop-first, but layouts should still adapt cleanly to smaller screens used in field or meeting contexts.
