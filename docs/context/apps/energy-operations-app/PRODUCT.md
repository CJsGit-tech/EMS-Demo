# Product

## Register

product-energy-operations

## User Profile

Primary users are EMS operators, energy analysts, dispatch planners, and commercial or business operators who manage generation, demand, storage, and bidirectional energy resources.

They need timely decision support, trustworthy forecasts, and clear operating constraints. Their work is sensitive to data quality, timing, and commercial impact.

## User Story

As an energy operations user, I want to forecast generation and demand, manage sell-power and charging or discharging behavior, and track energy resources so that I can operate the EMS with better commercial and operational outcomes.

## Product Purpose

The Energy Operations App supports the optimization and decision-making layer of the EMS-Demo platform. It focuses on forecasting, dispatch support, V2G or bidirectional charging logic, and energy resource commercialization.

Success means the user can see the operational picture, test energy decisions, and coordinate energy resources with enough clarity to support real operating decisions.

## Core Workflows

- Forecast generation and consumption.
- Evaluate sell-power opportunities and management actions.
- Model bidirectional charging and discharging behavior.
- Operate mobile bidirectional charger workflows.
- Track energy resource inventory and commercialization status.

## Scope Guidance

Included:

- Generation forecasting and optimization.
- Consumption forecasting and optimization.
- Sell-power prediction and management.
- Bidirectional charging and discharging business logic.
- Mobile bidirectional charger operating model.
- Energy resource inventory and commercialization.

Deferred or excluded:

- Site onboarding and telemetry integration.
- Internal ERP and document automation workflows.
- Full production-grade optimization models without validated telemetry.
