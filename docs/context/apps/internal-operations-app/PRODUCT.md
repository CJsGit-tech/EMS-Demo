# Product

## Register

product-internal-operations

## User Profile

Primary users are internal operations managers, office administrators, ERP coordinators, and support staff who handle work management, reports, documents, and internal data cleanup.

They usually work from a desktop browser inside the office, need clear task ownership, and care about auditability, handoff quality, and fast access to current operational records.

## User Story

As an internal operations user, I want to manage work requests, ERP-facing data, server or internal records, and generated reports in one place so that backoffice operations stay organized and the business can run with fewer manual steps.

## Product Purpose

The Internal Operations App supports the administrative and backoffice side of the EMS-Demo platform. It focuses on work management, ERP-adjacent data handling, internal process tracking, and document/report automation.

Success means a user can route internal work, generate outputs, and keep operational data clean without relying on ad hoc spreadsheets or manual database edits.

## Core Workflows

- Review and triage internal work items.
- Prepare or validate ERP-facing data.
- Manage internal records and server-related operational data.
- Generate reports, forms, and documents for routine business use.

## Scope Guidance

Included:

- Work management and internal task coordination.
- ERP-facing data preparation and exchange.
- Server or internal data optimization.
- Report, form, and document generation.

Deferred or excluded:

- Site telemetry intake.
- EMS forecasting and optimization logic.
- Field-device onboarding and site handoff workflows.
